About Me

I started college as an astrophysics major, chasing questions about the universe. Somewhere along the way, I realized the impact I wanted to make was closer to home — on Earth. Growing up with a Puerto Rican family gave me a front-row seat to what unreliable energy infrastructure actually costs a community, and that's what pulled me toward mechanical engineering.

I'm now a Mechanical Engineering and Physics student at Barnard College / Columbia University SEAS (4+1 B.A./M.S. program), still holding onto that early love of research while building toward a career in reliable energy and sustainability. That thread runs through everything here — from CAD design and manufacturing, to my work with the Building Energy Research Lab, to helping bring off-grid solar to communities in Puerto Rico with Let's Share the Sun.
